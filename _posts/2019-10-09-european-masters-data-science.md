*A European Approach to Master's Degrees in Data Science*, by Marco Piangerelli and Jeff Johnson.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/blog/tkb-1910-g.png" class="img-fluid" max-width="200px" alt="KDnuggets top stories badge" %}

Data science education in Europe has been re-evaluated and new recommendations, developed within the Erasmus+ project [Da.Re.](http://dare-project.eu), are leading the way to the next generation of data science Master's courses to better support and train students. The article was listed among the [KDnuggets top stories of October 2019](https://www.kdnuggets.com/2019/11/top-stories-2019-oct.html).
