*TOPDRIM project: taming data and complex systems with topology*, by Emanuela Merelli.

The FET project TOPDRIM opened a new line of research using a branch of mathematics, topology, to explore massive amounts of data looking for hidden patterns of correlated information. Two of the original researchers tell their story in the [Future Tech Week 2019 blog](http://www.topdrim.eu/FutureTech-Week2019/blog/index.html).
