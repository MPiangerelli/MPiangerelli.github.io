I am an Adjunct Professor (INF/01, Computer Science) at the [University of Camerino](https://computerscience.unicam.it) and an AI Engineer in the R&D division of [Vici & C. S.p.A.](https://vici.it), where I work on machine learning for optical metrology.

Born in Battipaglia (SA), Italy, in 1984, I hold a B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering from the [Politecnico di Milano](https://polimi.it) and an M.Sc. in Bioengineering from the [University of Bologna](https://www.unibo.it), where I worked on mathematical modelling of cardiac arrhythmias in collaboration with [Cornell University](https://cornell.edu) under the supervision of [Prof. Flavio Fenton](https://thevirtualheart.org/intro.html). I earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Camerino under the supervision of [Prof. Emanuela Merelli](http://www.emanuelamerelli.eu), with a thesis on topological data analysis for the study of epileptic seizures, carried out in collaboration with [ab medica s.r.l.](http://abmedica.it) In 2024 I was a visiting researcher at the Addis Ababa Institute of Technology, Addis Ababa University.

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning**, **topological data analysis** and **complex systems modelling**, with particular attention to *trustworthy AI*, *concept and data drift*, and *interpretable learning methods*. I have contributed to European, regional and industrial R&D projects including TOPDRIM, Da.Re., MIRACLE, SIMPLE, AI.EM, UNICAM 4 INGKA and Unicam 4 VICI (see the [projects](/projects/) page).

I am a member of the [Processes and Services (PROS) Lab](https://pros.unicam.it) and of the [Bioshape and Data Science Lab](http://www.emanuelamerelli.eu/bigdata/doku.php) at the University of Camerino, and an external member of the [Center for Philosophy Science and Policy](https://cpsp.univpm.it) at Università Politecnica delle Marche. I serve on the editorial boards of *Intelligenza Artificiale* (Sage), *PeerJ Computer Science*, *Applied Computational Intelligence and Soft Computing* (Wiley) and *Frontiers in Smart Manufacturing*, and I review for journals such as *IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering*, *IEEE TPAMI*, *Neurocomputing* and *Entropy*. I co-organise the SynDAiTE and DELTA workshop series on synthetic data and drift (see [events](/events/)).

I teach Machine Learning at the Master's and Ph.D. programmes in Computer Science at the University of Camerino (see [teaching](/teaching/)). My institutional page is on the [Unicam faculty directory](https://docenti.unicam.it/pdett.aspx?ids=N&tv=d&UteId=1039).
