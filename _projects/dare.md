**Website:** [http://dare-project.eu](http://dare-project.eu)

Da.Re. aimed to bridge the gap between the needs of businesses and the educational offer of higher education institutions by designing a European curriculum for a Master's degree in Data Science. Proposed by Unicam with Loccioni Group as lead partner, with IPB, The Open University, UP, Confindustria Ancona, Maisis, eConsulting, Nissatech, Abelium and VSL. Budget 418 k€. I was a member of the Unicam research unit and co-author of the project's Handbook of Machine Learning.
