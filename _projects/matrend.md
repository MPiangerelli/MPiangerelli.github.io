MATREND tested the use of geothermal plants in combination with other plants as a strategy for the energy upgrading of public and private buildings, and studied innovative energy storage systems. Partners: Unicam Geology, Computer Science and Physics divisions, Pensy, Geotermia Marche, Otto, Fullservice. I was a member of the Computer Science research unit.
