SIMPLE studies and develops products implementing predictive maintenance logic, connected to a platform that is highly flexible (applicable with minimal configuration to different products), low cost and non-invasive. Lead partner SIGMA S.p.A. with Clabo, Simonelli Group, Schnell and Pieralisi Maip. Unicam budget 288 k€. I was a member of the Unicam research unit for the unsupervised learning module, as a consultant to Sigma and Schnell.
