**Website:** [http://www.topdrim.eu](http://www.topdrim.eu)

TOPDRIM developed new mathematical and computational formalisms, guided by the topology of data, to describe the dynamics of complex multilevel systems and their emergent phenomena. Coordinated by Unicam with ISI Foundation, The Open University, University of Amsterdam, Aix-Marseille University and the University of Southern Denmark. Budget 2.46 M€ (Unicam share 468 k€). I was a member of the Unicam research unit.
