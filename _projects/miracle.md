MIRACLE created a laboratory of excellence to develop and test innovative and interoperable technologies for home automation and living environments, with three R&D projects: IoT for human safety, intelligent systems for environmental comfort and sustainability, and advanced solutions for human-system monitoring and interaction. Lead partner MAC S.p.A., with Unicam, UnivPM, INRCA and about twenty companies. Budget 8.5 M€ (Unicam share 815 k€). I was a member of the Unicam research unit.
