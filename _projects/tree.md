T.R.E.E. built an integrated, modular and expandable platform for delivering and monitoring personalised rehabilitation processes for subjects with chronic disabling outcomes of brain or spinal cord injury, movement disorders and mild cognitive impairment, both in specialised facilities and at home. Budget 1.4 M€. I was a member of the Unicam research unit for the behavioural analysis module, as a consultant to HP Composites, working with Prof. Renato De Leone.
